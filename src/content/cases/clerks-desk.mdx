Arthur Cross kept the books at Halloran & Sons, shipping agents, for eleven years. He was never late and never ill. On the last Friday of March 1934 he locked his desk at six, said good night to the porter, and was not seen again.

The police took one look and called it a man with debts. His sister didn't believe it. She has his desk, still locked, with everything exactly where he left it, and she has given you the key.

Inside are five things that don't belong in a clerk's desk. Arthur liked puzzles. Each of the five hides one word. Find all five, then open the last file.

Nothing here is labelled. Look at each one the way you learned at the start: what does it look like, and what's cheapest to try?
