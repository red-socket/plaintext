Radio Halloran broadcast pop music from an abandoned sea fort off the English coast for three summers in the 1960s, until the government closed the pirate stations down. Its best-loved voice was "Captain" Ray Marsh, who played records nobody else would and ended every night with a puzzle for his listeners.

Ray is eighty-six now. The Halloran listeners' club, down to a dozen members, has just received an envelope from him: a membership card, a playlist, and a link to three files. His note says he's holding one last reunion, and only people who can find it are invited.

He hasn't lost his touch. Each file hides one word. Find all five, then open the last file to work out where the reunion is.
