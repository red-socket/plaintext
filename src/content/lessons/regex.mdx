<Step n={1} title="Gaps and anchors">

A **regular expression** (regex) is a small language for describing text. In puzzles you mostly use it to search a word list for words that fit what you know.

- A letter matches itself.
- A **dot** `.` matches any one letter.
- `^` means the start of the word, and `$` the end.

So `^c.t$` means: c, any letter, t, and nothing else. The search below looks through 38,015 common English words.

<Step n={2} title="Choices and repeats">

- **Square brackets** pick one from a set: `[aeiou]` is any vowel, and `[^aeiou]` any letter that isn't one.
- A star `*` means "any number of the thing before, even none", a plus `+` means "one or more", and `{5}` means exactly five.

So `^[^aeiou]*$` is a word with no vowels, and `^.{12}$` is any twelve-letter word. And `.*`, any letters or none, lets a pattern sit anywhere inside a word: `.*qu.*` is every word containing *qu*. (The tool's *Whole words only* adds `^` and `$` for you, so remember the `.*` when you want a match in the middle.)

<QuickCheck id="regex-vowel" answer={["A VOWEL", "ANY VOWEL", "VOWEL", "ONE VOWEL", "VOWELS"]} note="Exactly one of a, e, i, o or u.">What does `[aeiou]` match?</QuickCheck>

</Step>

<Step n={3} title="Remembering a letter">

Round brackets **capture** what they match, and `\1` means "the same thing again". So `.*(.)\1.*` is any word with a double letter, and `^(.)(.).\2\1$` is a five-letter word that reads the same backwards: first letter equals last, second equals fourth.

</Step>

<Step n={4} title="Anagrams">

Patterns can't rearrange letters, so the tool has a separate anagram mode: give it letters and it finds every word that uses exactly those.

<QuickCheck id="regex-silent" answer="SILENT" note="Enlist, inlets, listen, silent and tinsel all use the same six letters.">Which anagram of LISTEN means quiet?</QuickCheck>

</Step>

<Step n={5} title="When to reach for it">

- A word with **some letters known** and some missing: crosswords, smudged notes, half-decoded messages.
- A clue about the **shape** of a word: double letters, no vowels, letters in order.
- A hunch you want to check: *is there really only one word like that?*

Puzzle hunters use big tools built on the same idea, like Nutrimatic and Qat, with lists of millions of words and phrases.

</Step>
