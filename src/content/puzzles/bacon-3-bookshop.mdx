hints:
  - "The sentence is ordinary. The capitals aren't."

  - "There are 25 letters. Read lowercase as A and capitals as B, in groups of five."
writeup: |
  The sentence means nothing special, but its capitals are random, which makes two styles of letter. Twenty-five letters is five groups of five: Bacon's cipher.

  Reading lowercase as A and capitals as B gives five groups that spell `SWORD`.

  Random capitals, mixed fonts or stray bold are almost always hiding something in two styles.
