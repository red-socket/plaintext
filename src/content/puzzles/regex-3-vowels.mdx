writeup: |
  The pattern puts a, e, i, o and u in order, with any non-vowels before, between and after: `^[^aeiou]*a[^aeiou]*e[^aeiou]*i[^aeiou]*o[^aeiou]*u[^aeiou]*$`. In the list, only one word fits: `facetious`.

  *Abstemious* also works, but it's too rare to make a common-word list. Patterns are only as good as the list they search.
