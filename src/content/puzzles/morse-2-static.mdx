  - "The first word is three letters: - .... . is THE."
writeup: |
  A tone switching on and off in two lengths is Morse. At the slow speed, the gaps between letters are stretched so each letter is easier to hear.

  Written down, it's `- .... . / .- -. ... .-- . .-. / .. ... / .- -. - . -. -. .-`: THE ANSWER IS ANTENNA.

  To transcribe sound, write dots and dashes first and decode afterwards. Doing both at once is how letters get lost.
