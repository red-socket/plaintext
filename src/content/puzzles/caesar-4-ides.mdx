writeup: |
  The letter says to use the date as the key, and it's dated the Ides of March: 15 March, the day Caesar was killed in 44 BC. The shift is 15.

  Shifting back by 15 (or forward by 11, since 15 + 11 = 26) turns `IWT UXGHI QADL` into `THE FIRST BLOW`. The letter names Casca, the senator said to have struck first.

  The key wasn't in the puzzle as a number. You had to know, or look up, what the date meant. Real puzzles do this a lot: when a puzzle says "the key is…", look for something that can become a number.
