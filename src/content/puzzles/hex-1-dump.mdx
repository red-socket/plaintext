  Only 0–9 and A–F, in pairs: hex. Each pair is one byte.

  `54 68 65` is The, `20` is a space, and the line reads `The answer is GLACIER`. The 6s and 7s are lowercase; the 4s and 5s at the end are capitals.

  In a hex dump, 20 between groups is the space. It splits the text into words before you decode anything.
