  - "A double letter is (.)\\1 as a pattern."
  - "Three double letters in a row is three of those back to back, each with its own number."
  - "Search for .*(.)\\1(.)\\2(.)\\3.* (the .* lets anything come before and after) and pick the shortest match."
writeup: |
  A double letter is `(.)\1`; three in a row, each with its own capture, is `(.)\1(.)\2(.)\3`, with `.*` either side to allow the rest of the word. Only three words in the list match: bookkeeper, bookkeepers and bookkeeping. The shortest is `BOOKKEEPER`, with oo, kk and ee.
