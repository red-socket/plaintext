  The signature J.C. points to Julius Caesar, and III in the corner is the shift: 3, the one Caesar himself is said to have used.

  Shifting every letter back by 3 turns `ZLVK BRX ZHUH KHUH` into `WISH YOU WERE HERE`, and the rest of the card says the answer is AQUEDUCT.

  Puzzle makers rarely hand you the key this openly, but they often leave it somewhere nearby: a number in a corner, a date, a name.

A postcard turns up in a second-hand book, a picture of a Roman bridge on the front. On the back, one line of capitals, signed **J.C.**, with **III** pencilled small in the corner.
