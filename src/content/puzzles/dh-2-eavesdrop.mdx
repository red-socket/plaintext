writeup: |
  You only saw the public numbers, but with p = 89 there are only 88 possible secrets. Trying each, 3⁴¹ mod 89 = 56, so Alice's secret is 41. The shared secret is then 69⁴¹ mod 89 = 9, and shifting back by 9 gives `NOBODY CAN HEAR US. THE ANSWER IS WHISPER.`

  That's why real key exchanges use primes hundreds of digits long: the same search would take longer than the age of the universe.
