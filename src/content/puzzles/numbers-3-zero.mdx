hints:
  - "Decode it as A1Z26. Does it look almost right?"
  - "There's a 0 in it, and A1Z26 has no letter for 0. Who counts from zero?"
  - "Programmers do. Use A = 0: 19 is T, 7 is H, 4 is E."
writeup: |
  Read as A1Z26, the first word comes out `SGD`, one letter before THE, and the 0s don't decode at all. Both are signs that the count starts at zero.

  With A = 0, `19-7-4` is THE, and the note reads `THE PASSWORD IS BOOTSTRAP`.

  A 0 among small numbers, or a decode that's off by one letter everywhere, means A0Z25.
