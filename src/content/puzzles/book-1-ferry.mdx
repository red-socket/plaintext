writeup: |
  Pairs of small numbers next to a page of text: a book cipher. Each pair is a line and a word on that line.

  `1.1` is The, `2.3` is captain, `4.6` is carried, and `6.5` is key. The numbers say `THE CAPTAIN CARRIED THE KEY`, whatever the story says.

  When a puzzle shows you a page and some numbers, count lines and words first. It's quicker than anything clever.

A page torn from a novel, found folded in a ferry timetable. Pencilled under the last line, a short list of numbers.

According to the numbers, who had the key?
