writeup: |
  Every pixel is grey 128, except the ones that make up a word, which are 129: one step lighter, too little to see. Stretching the contrast turns 128 into black and 129 into white, and so does the lowest bit plane. The word is `OTTER`.

  A suspiciously flat image is worth stretching. Nothing is that flat by accident.
