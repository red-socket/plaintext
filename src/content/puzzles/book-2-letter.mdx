  The numbers can't be line numbers: some are bigger than the letter's five lines. Counting words through the whole letter instead, and letters within each word, works: it's a word.letter book cipher, and the key is the letter itself.

  The seven references spell `ORCHARD`.

  When the obvious scheme gives numbers that are too big, try a scheme that counts something smaller, words instead of lines, letters instead of words.
